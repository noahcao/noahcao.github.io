Miscellaneous

During my free time, I enjoy hiking, traveling, photography, reading and playing badminton.

Photography

I studied painting and sketch since my very young childhood for more than 10 years. But now I only occasionlly do that as I find that spending hours without interruption becomes ever difficult to me. I take photography as an alternative since my undergraduate years. I shoot both digital and films. I learned a lot about photography from the digital photography lectures by Marc Lovey. I also share my photos through instagram (@jinkuncao).
